# Phase 0 story lock

## Approved narrative for later phases

The landing-page story remains:

1. India as the hero.
2. The scale of watershed evidence.
3. The triage question.
4. India to Maharashtra.
5. Maharashtra to Nashik.
6. One illustrative priority structure.
7. “But how does BHU-DRISHTI know?”
8. Normal-scroll explanation of evidence fusion, explainability, human review,
   prioritisation, impact, workflow, product modes, integration, and outcomes.
9. Return to India and close.

Only steps 1, 4, and 5 are represented in the Phase 1 geometry spike. It does
not include markers, watershed polygons, programme metrics, dashboard panels,
the final pinned timeline, or the full landing-page sections.

## Research-updated opening copy for the full landing page

- Headline: “From geo-coded evidence to watershed decisions.”
- Subtext: “A proposed decision-support layer that aligns field photographs
  with satellite, rainfall, and terrain evidence—so officials can see what
  changed, what remains uncertain, and what to verify next.”
- Prompt: “Scroll to explore ↓”

This copy superseded the Phase 0 wording after the 8 September 2026 research
review in [`report-source.md`](./report-source.md). The story sequence remains
unchanged; the claims are now more specific and evidence-bounded.

The geometry spike uses implementation-focused explanatory copy so it cannot be
mistaken for the finished hero.

## Navigation and destinations

The complete header navigation and CTAs are intentionally absent from the spike.
For Phase 2, About, How It Works, and Impact will target real in-page sections.
Until application routes exist, Login, Enter Command Centre, Explore Demo, and
View Architecture must be presented as explicit preview states rather than dead
or misleading links. Adding real routes is outside Phase 0/1.

## Claim guardrails

- Do not call district geometry a watershed boundary.
- Do not claim current integrations, live inference, or an operational command
  centre.
- Describe possible visible issues and recommended verification, never a
  definitive structural diagnosis.
- Describe BHU-DRISHTI as decision support that augments officials and existing
  systems.
- Keep the prototype boundary warning visible anywhere this snapshot is shown.
